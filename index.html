THE PLACES THAT SHAPE US

Our journey, so far.

Every place adds a new chapter. Every chapter leaves us with something to carry forward.

Universitas AtmaJaya
Unika Atma Jaya Semanggi

Our journey at Atma Jaya began with a room full of possibilities and people we had yet to know. Along the way, we got to meet people beyond BLI, share moments with people from different backgrounds, make new connections, and somehow turn strangers into best friends. With a welcoming environment, supportive lecturers, and facilities that made learning more enjoyable, each day brought something new to discover. This chapter has already given us memories worth keeping, but our time at Atma Jaya is far from over. There are still more moments to create, more places to discover, and more stories waiting to unfold.

BLI
BCA Learning Institute

August 19 marked the beginning of STELLARS chapter at BLI. What started with unfamiliar faces slowly turned into open conversations and shared laughs. Somewhere between classes, and little breaks, the awkwardness faded and comfort found its way in. Maybe it was never about the big moments, but the little ones that made this chapter worth remembering.

RTB
Rumah Talenta BCA

More than just a place to stay, Rumah Talenta BCA is where we grow, learn, and create unforgettable memories together. A home away from home, where friendships are built and dreams begin.

A LITTLE ABOUT US

More than a name.
It's our constellation.

STELLARS is our little universe—a group of BP11 learners brought together by one journey, while each carrying a story, personality, and ambition of their own.

Like stars in a constellation, we do not have to shine in the same way to belong together. We learn from different perspectives, find our rhythm as a team, and make room for everyone to grow.

From our days at Unika Atma Jaya and BCA Learning Institute to the moments we share at Rumah Talenta BCA, this space keeps our people, progress, and memories in one orbit.
